CASE NOTES — sports betting

Since legalization of sports betting, illegal markets have dropped by a third. Cohen '25 explains that the share of illegal sportsbooks fell from 36 percent to 24 percent.

Federal regulation is not automatically the answer. States should retain authority to experiment because local enforcement can respond faster to consumer-protection problems.

The other side says uniform federal standards improve safety. But state experimentation produces stronger protections in practice, since regulators can adapt rules to local markets.
